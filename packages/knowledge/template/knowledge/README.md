# Knowledge

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) before looking up or changing knowledge. Every session receives the article paths and descriptions, refreshed on each prompt.

Maintained articles live in `articles/`. Their provenance lives separately in `references/`; preserved material lives in `sources/` and `evidence/`. [origins.md](origins.md) records the systems and their access methods.

The knowledge package delivers the `knowledge-base` skill and checker. Ask to save material as a source or evidence, or to write findings up into an article. Existing free-form articles remain discoverable until you approve a migration.
