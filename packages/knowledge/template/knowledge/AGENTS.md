# Office knowledge

This base holds reusable understanding of the systems and subjects this office works with. The office owns these files and may add local rules below. Load the package's `knowledge-base` skill before changing them.

## Looking something up

1. Start from the articles and descriptions in the session context. Without that list, search `description:` lines under `articles/`.
2. Read the matching articles and relevant articles they link to.
3. Answer from those articles, citing the sections used. Open their linked `references/<subject>.md` when provenance or observation dates matter, or when checking a claim. Open sources or evidence when the articles do not answer the question or a check requires them. For current operational state, check the authoritative system.

## Local rules

- Suggest concrete durable additions or updates when they would save future research, preferably to an existing article. Write or capture only when asked or explicitly assigned.
- Re-check only when asked. Suspected staleness may prompt a suggestion, not an automatic refresh. There is no automatic curation or ongoing maintenance.
- "Save this" preserves a source or evidence. "Write this up" synthesizes maintained articles.
- Keep sources and evidence unchanged. Threads may grow at their end; later observations become new evidence.
- Back article claims with their references files. Cite files inside this base or origins in `origins.md`; copy session material into the base before citing it.
- After a change, run the skill's checker on this folder.
- Existing free-form articles remain readable until explicitly migrated. Read the skill's migration reference before converting them.
